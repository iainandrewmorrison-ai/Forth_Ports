AI Readiness for HR: Preparing Forth Ports for Practical, Responsible Copilot Adoption

Enlightened People is supporting Forth Ports with a Microsoft Copilot and AI training programme designed specifically for HR. Before we design anything, we want to understand your world — your workload, your data, your tools and your people — so the training is grounded in real work, not theory.

About the programme

Why this questionnaire matters

The best Copilot training is built around the tasks your team actually does. This short readiness assessment tells us where AI can genuinely help, what needs care around data and governance, and how confident people currently feel — so the scoping meeting and the training itself land on what matters most.

🧭

Not a test, not an audit

There are no right answers and no pass mark. The purpose is not to judge teams — it is to understand where support, training and governance are needed so we can meet you where you are.

🛠️

Built around real HR work

Your answers surface the repetitive tasks, documents and questions that eat your team's time — the raw material for hands-on Copilot exercises using your kind of work.

🛡️

Responsible by design

HR handles information about people. Data protection, fairness and human judgement run through everything we design — the assessment helps us get the governance right from the start.

🔒 A note on confidentiality: please do not submit confidential, personal, sensitive or identifiable employee information through this questionnaire. Describe tasks, patterns and processes — never named individuals or specific cases.
The programme

Copilot training designed around your real HR work

This is not generic AI training. Everything — the examples, the exercises, the governance guidance, the follow-through — is designed around how HR actually works at Forth Ports, including the differences between sites and operating contexts such as Grangemouth and Tilbury.

The training will be designed around

  • Real HR workloads — where your team's time actually goes, not hypothetical scenarios.
  • Current Microsoft Copilot access and usage — what you have today, and what needs to be in place before delivery.
  • Practical HR use cases — hands-on exercises using the kinds of documents and tasks your team handles every week.
  • Data protection and responsible AI — clear, workable guidance on UK GDPR, sensitive data and when human judgement must lead.
  • Confidence, capability and adoption barriers — meeting people where they are, from curious to cautious.
  • Differences between sites, teams and operating contexts — so examples feel relevant whether you are at Grangemouth, Tilbury or elsewhere.
  • Follow-through after training — champions, shared examples and support so the learning sticks rather than fades.
